# Channels — where Level 0 may scout

This document authorizes public research only. It does not authorize submissions,
outreach, account creation, community participation, or any other external action.

Priority allocation (30-day approx.) — keep as a living plan in the strategist:
- 20% competitor backlink-gap research
- 20% resource/listicle/roundup inclusion (highest-conviction)
- 15% discovery/product-listing sites + product-launch intakes
- 15% small high-relevance editorial outreach (low volume, personalised)
- 10% a linkable reference asset (deploy under Level-2)
- 5% community participation (genuine)
- 5% on a single discovery launch when ready
- 0% mass directories / paid / spam

## Concrete scout targets (start here)
- Editorial roundup / resource lists: Zapier-style best-tool lists, freelancer &
  contractor "best invoicing" roundups, accounting/bookkeeping resource pages, SMB/startup
  tool directories that curate genuinely.
- Product-discovery: legitimate tools/software listing sites with real users, product
  launch/community intakes.
- Community: relevant Reddit communities, HN/Indie Hackers — answer real questions
  naturally; never promotional auto-commenting. Founder-identity posts are Level-2.
- Broken/outdated replacement: find dead or obsolete invoice tools linked under :
  resources for SMB/accounting/construction; propose replacement with a real value.

## Score 0–100 (recorded by `growth_db.py add-prospect`)
- relevance to our tool/audience (≤40)
- likelihood of real placement (≤30: is it curated? do they list similar tools?)
- traffic/authority of the prospect (≤20)
- ease/good-faith (≤10)
Reject scores ≤30. A high-authority page linking to a dead free invoice generator is
high-scoring only when the page is relevant, curated, and InvoiceWorkshop genuinely fits.

## NoLLM rule
Deterministic checks (URL liveness, sitemap health, placement verification, and metric
collection) run as reviewed scripts so their meaning remains stable and auditable.
