# Business Context

InvoiceWorkshop.com — free, no-signup, **browser-first** business-documentation suite.

## Product
- Document types: invoice, proforma invoice, quotation, estimate, work order, purchase
  order, receipt; plus construction and contractor invoice modes.
- Everything local: IndexedDB workspace (business profile, customers, catalog, drafts),
  client-side PDF (jsPDF), local backup export, clear-data.
- Unique converters: quotation→invoice, estimate→work order/→invoice, work-order→invoice,
  proforma→invoice, invoice→receipt — reuse without retyping.

## User
- Freelancers + independent SMBs (contractors, trades, consultants, small agencies) who
  want a professional document now, without an account in a full suite.

## Differentiation
- "No signup, but it remembers you." A persistent browser workspace + on-device privacy
  a SaaS account can't offer.

## Monetization
- Display advertising is the planned primary model. The objective is approximately
  $10K/month; required traffic and page-view volume remain assumptions until the site has
  enough observed geography, engagement, ad inventory, and RPM data to model honestly.

## Position
- SEO-first static product; the broad invoice-generator cluster is a North Star (long
  shot for a new domain), supporting wedges (proforma/quotation/estimate/work-order
  /purchase-order + construction/contractor verticals) + long tail are the near-term,
  winnable search surface.
