composites, no invented data. The invoice in the screenshots is a fictional
joinery firm billing a fictional coffee roaster; the numbers, per-line 20% tax

## What is still owner-only

A Product Hunt post needs a **personal** account, at least one week old on
launch day. That is a real human identity and is never fabricated, so it stays
with the owner. Nothing else is outstanding: tagline, description, maker comment
and topics are all in `breakout_destinations` under key `ph-launch`, and the
images are here.

Create the account by **8 September** to clear the one-week minimum comfortably.
