# Product Hunt — what only you can do

Launch: **Tuesday 15 September 2026**, live 12:01am PST.
Account created 4 September, so it is **11 days old** on launch day against a
one-week minimum. No reason to move the date.

Everything below is here because it needs a real person. Nothing on this list
is automated, and personal engagement never will be.

## Before the 15th (10 minutes, any day)

1. Sign in to Product Hunt and open the post form.
2. Paste the fields from `bundle_json` (`growth_breakout.py`, key `ph-launch`) —
   tagline, description, maker comment are all sized to the field limits.
3. Upload `icon-240.png` as the thumbnail and the five gallery images.
   **Lead with `gallery-3-receipt.png`.**
4. Link: `https://invoiceworkshop.com/?utm_source=producthunt&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ph-launch-2026-09-15`
   Verified on 4 September: those parameters do reach GA4 intact.
5. Topics: Productivity, Finance, Small Business. Confirm they exist in the
   picker; substitute the nearest real ones if not. **Do not tick SaaS** — this
   is not SaaS, and the audience most likely to check is the one reading.
6. Pricing tag: Free.
7. Schedule for 15 September. **Do not publish early.**

## On the 15th

1. Post the maker comment as the first comment. It is written in your voice and
   is in the bundle under `maker_comment`.
2. Answer comments yourself, through the day. Questions about local-only storage
   are the ones most likely to come up; the honest answer is that documents live
   in `localStorage` and never reach a server, which is also why there is no
   account and no cross-device sync.

That is the whole list. No upvote solicitation, no coordinated engagement, no
paid hunter — Product Hunt removes products for exactly that, and it is not
something that will be done on your behalf.
